# Reflection

Among AWS, Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud Platform, the cloud platform that impressed me the most is Google Cloud Platform (GCP). I was impressed by its strong capabilities in Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and Kubernetes. Exploring the three platforms helped me understand that each provider has different strengths and services that can support various business needs.

AWS, Azure, and GCP have many similarities because they all provide cloud computing services such as virtual machines, storage, networking, identity management, databases, and Kubernetes. They also provide scalable and flexible resources that organizations can use without having to maintain all their physical infrastructure. Although their services have different names and features, their main purpose is to help organizations run applications, store data, and manage their IT resources through the cloud.

Business requirements greatly influence cloud platform selection. Organizations need to consider their budget, existing technologies, application requirements, scalability, performance, and future growth. For example, a company using many Microsoft technologies may benefit from Azure, while an organization focused on Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning may consider GCP. This shows that choosing a cloud provider should be based on the specific needs of the organization rather than simply choosing the most popular platform.

Acting as a Cloud Solutions Consultant taught me that cloud decisions require careful analysis. I learned that a good consultant must understand both the technical capabilities of cloud providers and the business requirements of clients. The goal is to recommend a solution that provides the best fit for a particular situation.

After completing this mission, my GitHub Cloud Computing Portfolio has improved because it now contains organized documentation, research, comparisons, recommendations, and evidence from the activities. It also helped me practice using Markdown and maintaining a professional and organized cloud computing portfolio.
